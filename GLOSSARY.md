# eShop

A small online store where shoppers browse a catalogue, fill a basket and place orders.

## Language

**Favourite**:
A product a signed-in shopper has marked to come back to later. It refers to the product only, with no quantity or remembered price.
_Avoid_: Wishlist item, saved item, bookmark, favorite

**Favourites**:
The set of Favourites belonging to one signed-in shopper.
_Avoid_: Wishlist, saved list
